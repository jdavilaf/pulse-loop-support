PULSE LOOP

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Languages and support · Contact support

No account is required. Progress is stored on your device. Usage analytics is optional and off by default. Non-personalized ads can still process technical data.

1. Controller and scope

For privacy questions or rights requests, contact spain.pulse.loop@gmail.com. This policy covers the iOS game, support communications and this website. Google, Apple and GitHub also have their own policies.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, scores, stars, moves, completed daily challenges, virtual currency, preferences and purchase status locally. No registration is required, and progress is not sent to a Pulse Loop server for synchronization. Some data may be included in iOS backups depending on your device settings.

Resetting progress or deleting the app affects local data; it does not delete Apple purchase records or information previously received by service providers.

3. Optional usage analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

Analytics is off by default. It is enabled only if you choose to share usage data in Settings > Privacy. You can keep playing with analytics disabled. Its purpose is to understand how the game works and improve difficulty, navigation and stability.

If enabled, Google may receive tutorial and gameplay events, level and game mode, moves, attempt duration, results, hint use, shop and virtual currency actions, purchases and advertising events. The SDK may also process device and app information, sessions and app-instance identifiers. These identifiers are pseudonymous; they do not guarantee anonymity.

In this version, Analytics collection of the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) is disabled, and Analytics consent for advertising storage and ad personalization is denied. We do not add your name or email address to gameplay events.

Disabling analytics stops future collection and resets local Analytics data and identifiers. It does not automatically delete data already sent to Google. Contact us with requests about that data; handling depends on whether records can be identified and on the provider’s available tools.

4. Advertising and consent: Google AdMob and UMP

Pulse Loop uses Google AdMob for interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized; we do not request ads selected from a profile of your interests. The app does not request tracking permission through App Tracking Transparency. This does not mean that no data is processed or that SDKs never use technical identifiers.

Google and applicable advertising providers may process IP addresses, approximate location derived from them, device and app information, permitted identifiers, ads viewed, interactions and diagnostic or performance data. Purposes may include ad delivery and measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention and service operation.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages consent messages and choices where applicable to your region and the service configuration. Ads are requested only when UMP indicates they are allowed. Available advertising choices can be reopened in Settings > Privacy. Rejecting a choice does not necessarily remove all ads: limited ad modes may be served where permitted.

The remove-ads purchase removes advertising interruptions. Rewarded videos you choose to watch may remain available. Advertising choices are separate from Analytics consent.

5. In-app purchases and support

Apple processes payments through the App Store and StoreKit. Pulse Loop does not receive your card details. The app uses transaction status and purchased products to deliver and restore purchases. Apple retains its records under its terms and privacy policy.

If you contact support, we process your email address and message to respond and resolve your request. Share only necessary information; do not send passwords, tokens or payment details.

6. Purposes, grounds and recipients

Local storage and purchase information are used to provide the game and purchased features. Optional analytics relies on your consent. Advertising consent is requested when required; technical, security and fraud-prevention processing depends on the service and applicable rules. Support communications are used to handle your request.

We do not sell your data. Necessary information may be processed through Google, Apple, the email provider used for support and GitHub for website hosting. Providers may operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Retention and international transfers

Game data remains on your device while the app and its data are retained, unless you use available reset controls. Support communications are retained for as long as needed to handle your request and applicable obligations.

Google and Apple retain received data according to its type, service settings and their policies. We do not claim it is deleted when you uninstall the game. Aggregate reports may be retained differently from events associated with an app instance.

Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States and other locations where they operate. Their policies describe applicable transfer conditions and safeguards. Contact us for additional information.

8. Your choices and rights

You can play without enabling Analytics, change your choice in Settings > Privacy, review available advertising options, reset game data and contact support. Withdrawing consent does not affect prior lawful processing.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability, withdraw consent and complain to the relevant authority. In Spain, this is the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es). Requests involving Google or Apple records may require their mechanisms and information that identifies those records. We do not require identity documents unless necessary and proportionate to verify a request.

9. Minors, security and this website

If you are a minor, ask a parent or guardian for help with privacy decisions and purchases where needed. An App Store age rating does not mean SDKs process no data. Contact support if you have concerns about information relating to a minor.

We apply consent and data-limitation controls and rely on providers’ security measures. No system offers absolute security.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may log technical information, such as IP addresses and requests, to operate and protect the service. These pages do not include our own website analytics system.
